# Blind review instructions

Open only this guide and run-02/blind.json before scoring. Someone who has already examined the labeled transcripts or mapping should disclose that exposure. Each record includes the source, two submitted claims, and the raw model answer. The output IDs and display order are randomized. Wording may still reveal which instruction was used.

Use the attached blank CSV. For each submitted claim record the conclusion the model actually gives. Use S for supported, R for contradicted or needs revision, U for insufficient evidence, and X for absent, ambiguous or mutually contradictory conclusions. An equivalent clear explanation may supply the finding even without a literal label. Ignore polish, length, and the model's guess about who wrote the submission. Do not repair an answer or substitute your own judgment for its conclusion.

Record rationale for difficult calls, disclose any perceived arm clues, and lock your completed CSV before opening the answer key or mapping. The scoring script compares those findings to the frozen key. Sign the scoring record with your chosen identifier, date, and any prior access to this work. This pilot does not authenticate reviewer identity.
